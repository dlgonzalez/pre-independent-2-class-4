Beyond words

We moved from talking about communication to considering the different elements that shape a message.

Before listening

What influences communication?

We discussed what can influence the way a message is sent and understood. Then we listened to an audio about four elements of communication.

01

Body language

Gestures, posture, facial expressions and other physical signals that accompany a message.

02

Tone of voice

The way our voice sounds can influence how listeners interpret our words.

03

Attitude

The attitude we communicate can affect the impression we create.

04

Words

The actual words and expressions we choose to communicate our message.

Main idea from the listening: Communication involves more than words. The audio highlighted the important role of body language alongside tone, attitude and verbal content.
Reflection

Think about it

Can the same words communicate different meanings depending on body language or tone of voice?

Suggested practice

English dictation

We explored Speechling as a resource for practising dictation. Choose a short activity, listen carefully, write what you hear, and check your answer.

Speechling · English Dictation
Listen, write, check and repeat.
Open practice ↗
1 · ListenListen to the complete sentence first.
2 · WriteWrite the words you hear.
3 · CheckCompare your answer with the correct version.
4 · RepeatListen again and notice what you missed.
Practice tip: Focus on small words, verb endings, contractions and word boundaries. These details are easy to miss when listening.
